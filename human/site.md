# AI Atlas

tagline:  Tech trees for the ideas AI
footer:   A working atlas, updated as I read. Notes are living documents, not finished essays.

I learn technical topics by reading papers, white papers and technical blogs. Each atlas
on this site maps one topic as a tech tree: the milestones, what each one unlocked, and how
they connect. Every milestone links to its primary sources, and every source has a page for
my reading notes and highlights, with deep links back to the exact page, section or sentence.

## How this site is made

The content lives in plain Markdown files in the `human/` folder. The engine that turns
them into this site lives in `engine/`. Coding agents extend the engine; I write the content.
